Data deletion instructions

Last updated: 26 September 2026

You can request deletion of personal information that Pandora's Box holds from its Facebook and Meta business connection. Pandora's Box is presented under the Banatao Systems brand.

How to make a request

  1. Email markjohnsonbanatao888@gmail.com with the subject Pandora Meta data deletion request.
  2. Tell us the Pandora organization name and the email address you use to sign in. If known, include the connected Facebook Page name or ID and describe whether you want the connection removed, Meta-sourced information deleted, or both.
  3. We will explain any steps needed to verify your identity and authority for that organization before acting. We will review the request, identify the information it covers, explain any applicable retention exceptions, and tell you when the requested action is complete.

Do not email passwords, access tokens, App Secrets, or identity documents. If additional verification is needed, we will provide a safer way to complete it.

Withdrawing Meta access

You may also remove Pandora's Box from the connected apps or business integrations in your Facebook account settings. This ends future access through that authorization, but it does not automatically erase information already stored by Pandora. Contact us using the steps above for deletion of data held by Pandora.

What the request can cover

A verified request may cover connected Meta user and business identifiers, Page and ad account metadata, permission and connection records, access tokens, and Meta-sourced content or drafts stored for the organization. The exact scope depends on what is held and whether the requester is authorized to act for that organization. Security or legal obligations may require limited retention; we will explain any exception that applies.

For questions about access or correction, use the same email address. Read the privacy policy for more about the Meta connection.
