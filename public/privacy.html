Privacy policy

Last updated: 26 September 2026

Pandora's Box is presented under the Banatao Systems brand. This notice describes information used to provide Pandora's Box, with particular detail about its optional Facebook and Meta business connection. For privacy questions, contact markjohnsonbanatao888@gmail.com.

Information we process

For Pandora accounts and organizations, we process the sign-in and membership information needed to identify the person and organization using the service, along with information users submit while using its features.

If an authorized organization administrator connects a Meta account, Pandora receives the Meta user ID and display name, granted permission names, accessible Facebook Page IDs and names, Page tasks, ad account IDs and names, ad account status and currency, and available business IDs and names. The authorization flow also returns user and Page access tokens. The connection can make Page, engagement, and advertising information available to features that an authorized user chooses to use, subject to the permissions granted in Meta.

Why we use it

We use these details to establish and verify the connection, identify the organization and assets it may access, provide requested Page and advertising reports or drafts, maintain account security, and respond to support and privacy requests. Connecting Meta alone does not launch a paid campaign or spend money.

Where it goes and who can access it

Pandora stores Meta access tokens in Supabase Vault and connection details in organization-scoped records. Its service runs on cloud infrastructure including Supabase and Vercel. Authorized people in the connected Pandora organization may see connection and business information that their role permits. Data needed for a requested feature may be processed by the service providers that deliver that feature, including AI processing if an authorized user asks Pandora to analyze connected information. Meta separately processes information within its own services under its policies.

How long we keep it

We keep connection information while needed to provide the authorized connection and related service, and review it when access is withdrawn or deletion is requested. Some information may need to be retained for security, legal, or operational reasons. Removal of Meta authorization stops future access but does not itself remove information already held in Pandora; use the instructions below to request that removal. We do not claim that all copies, including provider backups, disappear immediately.

Your choices and requests

You can review or withdraw Pandora's authorization in your Meta account settings. To request access to, correction of, or deletion of information held by Pandora, email markjohnsonbanatao888@gmail.com. We will verify the requester's identity and authority before disclosing or changing organization data. See the data deletion instructions for what to include. Never send passwords, access tokens, or other secrets by email.

Changes

We may update this notice when the service or its data practices change. The date at the top shows the current published version.
